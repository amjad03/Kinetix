catalogue.json lists PhET's HTML5 simulations (titles from PhET's own translations) and thumbs/
holds reduced screenshots of them, from PhET's open-source repositories
(https://github.com/phetsims). The simulations themselves are downloaded on demand, unmodified.

Indian syllabus (tools/phet/classification.json). PhET and the PhET logo are trademarks of the
University of Colorado Boulder; KINETIX is not endorsed by PhET. See docs/operations/phet.md.
