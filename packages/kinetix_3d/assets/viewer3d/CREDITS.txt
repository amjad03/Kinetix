KINETIX 3D models and viewer

Human anatomy models (heart, brain, digestive system, lungs, eye, excretory
system, skeleton, skull, teeth) are made from BodyParts3D,
(c) The Database Center for Life Science, licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/
KINETIX simplified the meshes, cut the vessels short, split the ventricle
wall into left, right and septum, and added names, notes and animations.
The model files derived from BodyParts3D are shared under the same terms.

The coastlines on the Earth model come from Natural Earth
(https://www.naturalearthdata.com), which is in the public domain.

The coronavirus model is "SARS-CoV-2 Virion (PathogenAR Version)" by NIAID,
NIH 3D (3DPX-014820), Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

The globe uses NASA's Blue Marble: Next Generation pictures (public domain).
The landforms are made from Terrain Tiles on AWS (SRTM and GMTED2010 heights
courtesy of the USGS and NASA, ETOPO1 from NOAA; public domain) and a NASA
Worldview/GIBS MODIS picture (public domain).

The compound microscope is "Omax compound microscope" by 3Donimus (Poly Pizza),
Creative Commons Attribution 3.0.

Downloaded models: woolly mammoth, Cosmic Buddha and Apollo 11 command module
from Smithsonian Open Access (CC0); Saturn V, Apollo Lunar Module and Hubble
from NASA 3D Resources (public domain).

The cells, neuron, shapes, atoms, molecules, solar system, Earth's layers,
volcano, orbitals, hybridisation, crystal lattices, electric circuit, AC
generator, transformer, simple machines, ear, nephron, engine, gears, lens
bench and laboratory apparatus are built in code by KINETIX, and so are the
simplified Taj Mahal, Qutub Minar, India Gate and Konark Sun Temple.
