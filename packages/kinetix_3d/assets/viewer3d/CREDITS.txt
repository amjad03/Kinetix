KINETIX 3D models and viewer

Human anatomy models (heart, brain, digestive system, lungs, eye, excretory
system, skeleton, skull, teeth, the outer ear, the kidney; downloaded: liver,
stomach) are made from BodyParts3D,
(c) The Database Center for Life Science, licensed under
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/
KINETIX simplified the meshes, cut the vessels short, split the ventricle
wall into left, right and septum, and added names, notes and animations.
The model files derived from BodyParts3D are shared under the same terms.

The inner ear (cochlea, vestibule and semicircular canals) is the micro-CT
surface of specimen F07 from "Human Bony Labyrinth: Co-Registered CT and
micro-CT Images, Surface Models and Anatomical Landmarks" by W. Wimmer,
L. Anschuetz, S. Weder, F. Wagner, H. Delingette and M. Caversaccio (2019),
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3355272, Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International. KINETIX simplified it, split it into its three parts and
placed it in the ear.

The DNA model's atoms are the B-DNA dodecamer of PDB entry 1BNA (H. R. Drew,
R. M. Wing, T. Takano, C. Broka, S. Tanaka, K. Itakura and R. E. Dickerson,
1981) from the RCSB Protein Data Bank (https://www.rcsb.org), CC0.

The coastlines on the Earth model come from Natural Earth
(https://www.naturalearthdata.com), which is in the public domain.

The coronavirus model is "SARS-CoV-2 Virion (PathogenAR Version)" by NIAID,
NIH 3D (3DPX-014820), Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International.

The globe uses NASA's Blue Marble: Next Generation pictures (public domain).
The landforms are made from Terrain Tiles on AWS (SRTM and GMTED2010 heights
courtesy of the USGS and NASA, ETOPO1 from NOAA; public domain) and a NASA
Worldview/GIBS MODIS picture (public domain).

The compound microscope is "Omax compound microscope" by 3Donimus (Poly Pizza),
Creative Commons Attribution 3.0.

Downloaded models: woolly mammoth, Cosmic Buddha and Apollo 11 command module
from Smithsonian Open Access (CC0); Saturn V, Apollo Lunar Module and Hubble
from NASA 3D Resources (public domain; KINETIX split them into named parts
and keeps each part's pictures); liver and stomach from BodyParts3D (above);
tooth, skin, bacterium and mitochondrion built in code by KINETIX.

The cells, neuron, shapes, atoms, molecules, solar system, Earth's layers,
volcano, orbitals, hybridisation, crystal lattices, electric circuit, AC
generator, transformer, simple machines, nephron, engine, gears, helical
and bevel gears, cam and follower, lens bench, laboratory apparatus, RCC
frame building, truss, arch, suspension and cable-stayed bridges and
footing types, the DC machine, three-phase induction motor, single- and
three-phase transformers and alternator are built in code by KINETIX, and so are the
simplified Taj Mahal, Qutub Minar, India Gate and Konark Sun Temple, the
middle ear and the inside of the kidney. The chemical engineering models
(alcohols, acids and sugars; CSTR, plug flow and packed bed reactors; shell-
and-tube heat exchanger; tray distillation column) and the electric-vehicle
models (battery pack, PMSM traction motor, drivetrain) are built in code by
KINETIX too.
