Common patterns found in the research
  • One floating toolbar, bottom centre (Teachmint, BrightClass, myViewBoard): pen, highlighter, eraser, select, shapes, tools, undo, redo, then smart tools and AI. Reachable by a short teacher standing at the panel.
  • Page controls bottom right: previous, "3 / 8", next, add page, page overview.
  • Class and session bottom left / top left: class name, Go live, attendance, record.
  • Content opens beside the board, not over it (Teachmint EduAI, BrightClass chat, myViewBoard split): the board stays visible and writable while the panel shows AI, 3D, video or a PDF; anything in the panel has Add to board.
  • Tool options open as a popover above the tool: pen type, thickness with live preview, colour row and a custom colour, single/multi-touch.
  • Geometry tools are real objects (Promethean, SMART): drag to move, handle to rotate, live angle and length readouts, draw along the edge.
  • Smart tools grid: timer, calculator, periodic table, graph, dictionary, picker, dice, spotlight, curtain, badges.
  • Subject templates and backgrounds: grids, lines, staff, maps, sports fields, colours.
  • Rewards (BrightClass badges) and quick quiz after a topic.

I could not find public material for Qeeb or MagicBox; if you have links or screenshots I'll fold them in.

  • Ruler, protractor, set squares (45° and 30-60°) and compass are objects on the board: drag to move, corner handle to rotate, two fingers to rotate and resize together.
  • Live readout of angle and length; snaps to 15°; scale in cm or inches, with the real physical size calibrated per panel.
  • Pen strokes near an edge draw a straight line along it; the protractor drag-arm marks an angle; the compass draws arcs and circles with a radius readout.
  • Lock, flip, resize, and a close button on each tool.
