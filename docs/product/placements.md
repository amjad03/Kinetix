# Placements, training, internships and alumni: user guide

For the placement officer, the principal, heads of department (read only), the IQAC (reports), students (Student App)
and alumni (alumni portal). ERP: **Placements & welfare → Placements**. Settings: **Settings → Placements**.

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| Offer policy | One offer and done, the dream ladder (a placed student sits only for a higher category), or open |
| Dream / super-dream lines (LPA) | A drive left without a category gets one from its package (0 switches a line off) |
| Require opt-in, debar days for absence | Students must opt in; skipping the first round of a registered drive debars them |
| Days to answer an offer | Without a deadline on the offer |
| Tell eligible students when a drive opens | On by default; once per drive |
| Documents needed | Resume; resume and class 10/12 marks cards; or all (with semester marks and ID proof) |
| Internship hours per credit, credits required | AICTE: 45 hours a credit; the programme's total (for example 14) |
| NATS minimum stipend (graduate, diploma), Government share % | From 1 April 2026: ₹12,300 and ₹10,900, share 50% |

## Recruiters

- **Recruiters** lists every company with its stage, the officer who looks after it and the next follow-up. Filter
  **Follow-up: due by today** each morning.
- On a company page, **Log interaction** records a call, email, meeting or campus visit, the outcome, the next step and
  a follow-up date. The **Interactions** tab is the company's history; **Mark followed up** closes a follow-up.
- **Recruiter log** shows every interaction across companies (import a spreadsheet of past calls there).
- Verify self-registered companies and issue a recruiter portal key from the company page.

## Drives

1. Add a drive (kit **Add**): company, role, package, window and criteria: minimum CGPA, most backlogs now, most subjects
   ever failed, class 10 and class 12 minimum %, most gap years, **Open to** (everyone, women only, men only), and
   **Documents verified first**.
2. **Open registration**: eligible students and their families are told. The **Eligible** tab lists every student of
   the programmes and batches with CGPA, backlogs, class 12 %, gap years, documents and the reason they are not eligible.
3. Add rounds (aptitude, GD, technical, interview, HR). For each round enter results by hand or **Import** the
   recruiter's spreadsheet saved as CSV (a roll number column; optional result and remarks; with no result column
   everyone listed is shortlisted). **Check** shows each line's problem; **Record** saves the good lines. Students are
   told their result.
4. Schedule interview slots, make offers to those through the final round, attach offer letters, record responses and
   joining.

## Documents

**Documents** is the verification queue: open the file, **Verify** or **Reject** with a reason. Students upload from the
Student App (**Careers → My placement → Documents**); a file the cell uploads is verified at once. A registration keeps
the resume on file.

## Internships and apprenticeships

- Internships take hours, kind (industry, research, in-house, rural, virtual) and mode; credits follow the hours unless
  you enter them. The **Internship credits** report shows who is short.
- **Apprenticeships** (NATS): add the graduate with the establishment, NATS enrolment and the monthly stipend (refused
  below the statutory minimum). The Government share is worked out. Record **Contract registered** with the contract
  number, **Started**, **Completed** with the certificate of proficiency. Open an apprenticeship to record each month's
  payment and the DBT credit.

## Training

Plan programmes (aptitude, technical, soft skills, mock interviews) for a programme and batch, add sessions, mark
attendance and enter test scores. Students see their attendance and scores in **My placement**.

## Alumni and referrals

Students ask a mentoring alumnus for a referral from the Student App; the alumnus answers in the alumni portal and the
student is told. The cell follows them under **Referrals** and can mark **Referred**, **Hired** or **Close**.

## Reports

**Placements → Reports** (also open to the IQAC): by programme (placement %, median, average and highest package),
NIRF graduation outcome (three graduating years with intake, lateral entry, minimum time, placed, median salary, higher
studies), **NAAC 5.2** (five outgoing batches: 5.2.1 placed, 5.2.2 higher education, 5.2.3 exams, with averages),
**AISHE** (outgoing batch by programme, all and women), by company, offer list, internships, internship credits,
apprenticeships, progression, training and feedback. Each downloads as CSV or Excel. **Placement brochure** downloads
the year's brochure (PDF) for recruiters.

## Student App

**Profile → Careers**: open drives with eligibility (and the reason in words when not eligible), register, withdraw,
answer offers. **My placement**: where you stand, documents with upload, every drive's rounds with the interview slot,
offers, training, internship credits, apprenticeships and referrals. **Career preparation → Resume → Fill from my
record** drafts the resume from the record.
