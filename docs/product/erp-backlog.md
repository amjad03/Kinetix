# ERP backlog (after the Board is complete)

Agreed with the product owner (2026-10-05): finish the Board first for the SIMS pilot
(Soundarya Institute of Management and Science, Bangalore University), then build these in
KINETIX ERP. Board, ERP and the Teacher, Parent and Student apps stay separate products in one
ecosystem.

Academic
- Admissions: online enquiry, application form, documents, application fee (Razorpay), merit
  lists, admit to a class.
- Exams and grading: Bangalore University scheme (internal assessment, credits, SGPA/CGPA, hall
  tickets, results); CBSE/PU report cards for later institutions.
- Outcome-based education (OBE): course outcomes, CO-PO/PSO mapping, attainment from marks.
- Certificates and ID cards: transfer, bonafide, conduct, study certificates; QR ID cards.
- NAAC / IQAC: AQAR criteria data from what KINETIX records, with export.

Operations
- HR and payroll: staff records, staff attendance (biometric/app), leave, payroll with
  PF/ESI/PT/TDS, payslips.
- Transport: routes, stops, bus fees, live bus location from the driver's phone.
- Hostel: rooms, allotment, fees, in/out register, mess.
- Placements and alumni: companies, drives, applications, offers; alumni directory.

Later: a trust-level group account (Soundarya Educational Trust runs a CBSE school, a PU college,
degree and law colleges and SIMS).
