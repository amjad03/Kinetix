# Academics and timetable: user guide

How a college plans its teaching week in ZYNETIX, keeps it current through the semester, and records what was
taught. The screens are in the ERP under **Timetable** and **Academics**; teachers work in the Teacher App and on the
smart board; students and parents see the result in their apps.

## Before the timetable

1. **Structure** (Academics → Structure): departments, programmes, classes (sections) and courses. Each course has its
   credits and contact hours a week as L-T-P (lectures, tutorials, practicals) and its type (core, elective, open
   elective, lab, project, skill).
2. **Planning** (Academics → Planning): regulations (schemes), elective and open-elective baskets with how many a
   student chooses, prerequisites, credit transfers (ABC, SWAYAM, NPTEL) and **faculty allocation**: who teaches each
   course to each class, or to one lab batch of it, for how many hours a week.
3. **Course registration** (CBCS): the registration window, seats per elective, students' ranked preferences,
   allotment by CGPA, first come or a weighted rule, the waitlist and advisor approval.
4. **Timetable setup** (Timetable → Setup):
   - *Bell schedule*: the periods and breaks of a day, for all programmes or one.
   - *Batches*: split a class into lab or tutorial batches (B1, B2).
   - *Unavailability*: the weekly times a teacher cannot be timetabled (a research afternoon, a day at the university,
     a course until a date). Teachers can add their own; a head of department keeps their department's.
   - *Special classes* and *compensatory working days* (a Saturday that follows Monday's timetable).
5. **Academics settings → Timetable**: the most periods a teacher may have a day and a week, the most in a row, and
   how many periods make one lab block.

## Timetable studio

Timetable → **Studio** keeps versions of the timetable. A version is a draft until it is published.

- **New version**: pick the classes (take every class of a term together, so electives can run in parallel for all of
  them), and start by generating, by copying the live timetable, or empty. A course taught to two classes together can
  be marked *taught together*.
- **Generating** places, for each class, every course's lectures and tutorials (L and T hours), its labs as blocks of
  consecutive periods that do not cross a break (one block per batch, the batches side by side in different labs when
  they have different teachers), combined classes once for all their classes, and each elective basket's options in
  the same period for every class, each with its own teacher and room. It keeps every teacher within their daily,
  weekly and consecutive limits and out of their unavailable times, picks the smallest free room that seats the class
  (a lab for a lab), spreads a course over the week, and leaves alone what other classes already hold. What it could
  not place is listed with the reason (teacher unavailable, over the load limit, no room, no free period, no teacher
  allocated).
- **Editing**: drag a period to another cell. The cell turns red over a clash (class, teacher or room) and the move is
  checked again on drop against the version and the live timetable of other classes, unavailability and room seats.
  A lab block or a parallel elective moves together. *Move* does the same from the keyboard; *Lock* keeps a period
  where it is when the version is generated again; *Remove* takes it out.
- The version page lists every clash left, the teaching load of each teacher (here and elsewhere, against the limits)
  and **what publishing changes** for each class (periods added, removed, kept).
- **Publish** (principal or administrator) makes the version the live timetable from a date. The classes' old periods
  are archived, not deleted, so attendance, lesson plans and recordings stay with what was taught. The version it
  replaces becomes *superseded*; elective periods are set on the term's course offerings so registration checks
  clashes against them; teachers, students and parents are told. Publishing is refused while a clash remains.
- **Make a copy** to change a published version, or to go back to an older one (copy it and publish the copy).
- **PDF** prints the week as a grid (landscape A4, signature lines) for a class, a teacher or a room, from a version or
  from the live timetable (Timetable → PDF).

The live timetable (Timetable) is still editable period by period for small changes; it refuses a period when the
teacher is unavailable. Substitutes are arranged on the same page (and from staff leave).

## The academic calendar

Holidays, exams and events by audience (institution, campus, programme, class, department), with drafts that only
staff see until published. Publishing a holiday tells students and families, cancels the substitutions and special
classes arranged for those days (their teachers are told) and reports how many periods will not meet. Timetable →
Setup → Working days counts the working days month by month and checks each term and the year against the working-day
minimum of the affiliating university or board (Settings → Affiliation): 90 a semester and 180 a year for the Karnataka
state universities (UGC norms), 220 a year for school boards.

## Teaching diary (work done)

After a period, the teacher writes its diary entry in the Teacher App (the book-pen icon on the period card) or on the
smart board (Today's plan → Save to diary): the syllabus topics taught (the lesson plan's are ticked to begin with),
how it was taught, what was done and, when it fell behind the plan, why. The topics are marked as covered for the
class. A substitute writes the diary of the period they covered.

In the ERP, **Teaching diary** (heads of department, the principal, the exam office):

- *Syllabus completion*: every class and course with topics covered, how far the term has run, periods met against the
  term's periods, entries behind plan, entries to review, and the pace (ahead, on track, behind).
- *Diary entries*: every entry on the record kit (filters, saved views, export, history); select entries and
  **Review** them, with a remark the teacher is sent.
- *Course register*: one class and course: hours allotted for the term (L-T-P × weeks) against periods taken, coverage,
  lesson plans, how the entries went against the plan, and the periods of the last two weeks still to log.

The course file (Course files) now has a cover with credits, L-T-P and faculty, the NBA checklist (syllabus, course
outcomes, timetable, lesson plans, teaching diary, 90% coverage, attendance, assessments, attainment, material), the
course outcome statements and the teaching diary.

## In the apps and on the board

- **Teacher App**: the day's periods include the ones the teacher covers for a colleague (*Covering for …*) and say
  when a colleague covers theirs; lab periods show the batch; the diary is written from the period card.
- **Smart board**: Today's plan shows today's periods in the room, the one on now highlighted and substitutes named;
  the signed-in teacher's current period is opened automatically (a substitute's too, and on a compensatory working
  day); topics are marked taught from Books and the diary saved from the board.
- **Student and parent apps**: More → Timetable shows the week as the student has it: their lab batch, their elective
  (once registered), substitutes, extra classes, holidays and the weekday a compensatory working day follows.
